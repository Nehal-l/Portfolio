Graphic Design Intern – GeoJuristoday | JULY 2025 – NOV 2025

Legal Awareness • Informative Posters • Social Campaigns • Branding

As a Graphic Design Intern at GeoJuris Today, a digital platform focused on delivering insights related to geopolitics, international relations, law, and current affairs, I was responsible for creating visually structured posters and informational creatives that prioritized awareness, factual accuracy, and content clarity. The organization regularly publishes research-driven and analysis-based content, requiring designs that effectively translate dense, information-heavy topics into visually engaging and easily digestible formats. In this role, I transformed complex geopolitical and legal concepts into clean, reader-friendly visual narratives by applying strong layout structuring and visual hierarchy principles. I ensured that each creative aligned with the organization's branding standards, tone, and formatting guidelines while maintaining consistency in typography, color palettes, spacing, and alignment. My responsibilities included designing social media creatives, awareness posters, and informational graphics that enhanced audience engagement and improved content readability. I collaborated closely with content writers and editors to ensure that the visuals accurately reflected the intended message and maintained factual integrity.
